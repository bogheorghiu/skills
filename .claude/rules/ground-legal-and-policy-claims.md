# Ground legal and policy claims in the primary record

Users rely on `reference/legal.md` and the sources' `caveats` to decide whether to run a scraper at all, so an error there costs them more than any other error in the repository.

- Quote the fact's own record: the judgment's operative part on EUR-Lex, the docket entry, the licence text, the service's own policy page. A blog post, a summary or your memory can point you to a source; it cannot stand in for one.
- Give each claim a tag (GROUNDED / UNRESOLVED / CONTRADICTED, or Gap) and the date you read the page. Policies change without notice, and an undated claim cannot be re-checked.
- Keep the file's candour. Where the law is unsettled or unknown, say so; the skill tells the user exactly that, and a confident-sounding line here becomes false reassurance there.
- `reference/legal.md` is a core file: owner approval in the conversation, then a PR (see `CLAUDE.md`).
